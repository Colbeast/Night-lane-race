Night Lane Race

Race 2 laps. Cars have real weight, so ramming and spinning rivals out works. Your car speeds up by itself, so you only steer and brake. Win races to earn coins, then spend them in the Garage.
P1: A / D, S brakes (or hold left/right on touch). P2: arrow keys. P3: J / L, K brakes. P4: 4 / 6, 5 brakes. Game controllers work too.
